## Product objective

Build one local workspace where the owner can create, edit, inspect, undo, reopen, play and export their work, with assistance grounded in authorized evidence. PLATZ Core owns canonical state and typed edits. Studio owns the authoring interface and Runtime will play pinned snapshots. ATLAS will read authorized evidence and propose edits through the same typed operations. The trusted host controls scope and commits. The first slice exercises manual editing and persistence on a public scene with two identically named objects. It advances U0 and U1.
